Domestic & International Logistics Management

Embassy Freight Systems USA, based out of Boston, MA has emerged as a leading provider in shipping solutions and Domestic & International logistics management.

Our mission since 2000 has been to help businesses streamline and expand their supply chain operations by providing innovative, customized, and trustworthy solutions to their shipping needs. Our complete suite of services includes Freight Forwarding, Import & Export Compliance, Customs Brokerage, Domestic Trucking, and Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Distribution & Warehousing.

We have meticulously cultivated a robust global network of partners over the last 20 years that enables us to offer cost-effective and multi-modal transportation management solutions, thereby ensuring the seamless flow of your goods across borders.
