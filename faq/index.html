What is freight forwarding and how does it work?

Freight forwarding is the process of organizing and coordinating the transportation of goods from one destination to another. It involves selecting suitable transportation methods, managing shipping documentation, coordinating cargo movement and supporting customs clearance to ensure an efficient shipping process.
