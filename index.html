FAQ

GENERAL

What is freight forwarding and how does it work?

Freight forwarding is the process of organizing and coordinating the transportation of goods from one destination to another. It involves selecting suitable transportation methods, managing shipping documentation, coordinating cargo movement and supporting customs clearance to ensure an efficient shipping process.

What services does your freight forwarding company offer?

Bahmas Transportation offers a range of freight and logistics services, including air freight, sea freight, overland transportation and customs brokerage. We also provide freight forwarding, import and export documentation, cargo handling and other logistics support to meet different shipping requirements.

What types of cargo can your company handle?

We handle various types of cargo, including general, bulk and specialized shipments. Our freight solutions accommodate different cargo requirements through air, sea and overland transportation, with appropriate handling and coordination.

How long does it take for my goods to be delivered?

Delivery time depends on several factors, including the shipment's origin and destination, transportation method, cargo type and customs clearance requirements. Contact our team with your shipment details to receive an estimated delivery timeframe.
