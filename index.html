Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is freight forwarding and how does it work?

Freight forwarding is the coordination and transportation of goods from one location to another using different modes of transport. It involves arranging shipments, coordinating transportation, managing documentation and supporting the movement of cargo.

What services does Bahmas Transportation Services offer?

Bahmas provides air freight, sea freight, overland freight and customs brokerage services. We support businesses with cargo transportation, import and export documentation and logistics coordination.

What types of cargo can Bahmas handle?

Our listed freight services include options for general cargo, oversized and overweight shipments, dangerous goods and temperature-controlled cargo. Suitability depends on the shipment requirements and applicable regulations.

How long does it take for my goods to be delivered?

Delivery time depends on factors such as the destination, transportation mode, shipment details and applicable customs procedures. Contact our team with your shipment requirements to discuss an estimated transit time.

How can I contact Bahmas for assistance?

You can reach out to Bahmas Transportation Services through the Contact page or Get a Quote option on our website to discuss your shipping and logistics requirements.

Shipment

How can I track my shipment?

For information about your shipment's progress, contact Bahmas Transportation Services with your shipment details. Our team can assist with the relevant shipment information.

Does Bahmas offer cargo insurance?

Bahmas lists cargo insurance as an option within its freight services. Contact our team to discuss available insurance options and coverage requirements for your shipment.

How do I know if my shipment requires special handling?

Certain shipments, such as oversized, overweight, dangerous goods or temperature-controlled cargo, may require special handling. Contact our team to discuss your cargo specifications and transportation requirements.

What happens if my shipment is lost or damaged during transit?

If your shipment is lost or damaged, contact Bahmas Transportation Services with the shipment details. Our team can help you understand the appropriate next steps and any applicable insurance arrangements.

Can Bahmas handle oversized or overweight shipments?

Yes. Bahmas lists oversized and overweight cargo transportation among its air, sea and overland freight service options. Contact our team to discuss your specific shipment requirements.

Can Bahmas assist with customs clearance for my shipment?

Bahmas provides customs brokerage support, including assistance with import and export documentation and customs-related coordination.

International

Does Bahmas provide international shipping services?

Yes. Bahmas Transportation Services provides international freight forwarding solutions through air, sea and overland transportation, helping businesses move cargo across international destinations.

What documents do I need to provide for international shipments?

International shipments may require documents such as a commercial invoice, packing list and other applicable shipping or customs documentation. Requirements depend on the cargo, origin and destination country. Contact our team for guidance on your shipment.

Which countries does Bahmas provide freight forwarding services to?
