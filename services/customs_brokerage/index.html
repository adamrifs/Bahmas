CUSTOMS BROKERAGE SERVICES

Smooth Customs Clearance with Reliable Documentation Support

Bahmas Transportation Services provides customs brokerage support to help businesses manage the documentation and procedures involved in importing and exporting goods. We assist customers with customs-related requirements and coordinate the necessary documentation to support smooth cargo movement.

From customs clearance documentation to import and export requirements, we focus on providing professional assistance, accurate coordination and responsive support. Our goal is to help customers navigate customs procedures while minimizing avoidable delays in the shipping process.

Included with Our Customs Brokerage Services

Customs Clearance

Support with the customs procedures required for importing and exporting goods.

Customs Bond

Support with customs bond requirements related to the payment of duties and fees.

Valuation & Coordination

Assistance with determining the appropriate tariff classification and value of goods.

Free-Trade Zone

Support with customs procedures for applicable free-trade zones and duty exemptions.

Importer Security Filing

Advance shipment information required for applicable import security procedures.

Partner Government Agency Filing (PGA)

Documentation support for government agencies regulating specific imported goods.

Import License Clearances

Documentation support for goods requiring specific import licenses.
